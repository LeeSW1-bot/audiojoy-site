Privacy

This covers the mailing-list form on this site. It is the only thing here that collects anything about you. There are no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking cookies.

What I collect

Your name, your email address, the option you pick under “I’m here for”, and anything you write in the note field. Nothing else, and nothing you haven’t typed in yourself.

Why

So I can tell you when the songs are finished and who ended up singing them, and so I can reply if you’ve asked me something. That is all. I don’t sell it, share it, or add you to anything else.

Lawful basis

Consent, under UK GDPR. You give it by sending the form, and you can withdraw it whenever you like.

Where it’s stored

The form is handled by Formspree, Inc., a form service based in the United States, which passes your message on to me by email. Your details are therefore transferred outside the UK and are held both by Formspree and in my email account. Formspree’s own privacy policy is at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

How long

Until you ask me to remove it, or until the list is no longer being used — whichever comes first.

Getting it deleted

Send a note through the form saying you want to be removed, or simply reply to any email you get from me. I’ll delete your details and won’t ask why.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask for a copy of what I hold about you, ask me to correct or delete it, or withdraw your consent. If you think I’ve handled your details badly, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
