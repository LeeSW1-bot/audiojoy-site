Newsflash · October 2026

Someone Somewhere is moving to the new year.

It was due on 6 November. It’ll now be January, possibly February.

The honest reason is that the song changed. What began as one idea has become something else — which is the best kind of surprise, and also the kind that needs more time. Rushing it to hit a date would serve the date, not the song.

The charity beneficiary for the Song 4 Pippa mix is being reconsidered too. We’ve found the charity we want to support and we’re waiting on their agreement, so we’re not naming it yet. The moment it’s confirmed, it’ll be here.

Anyone on the list hears the new date first.

Shook By The Look

An anthem of sass

A co-write from 2023 — and the only song the three of them made together. Kieran created the original music, wrote a verse, and sang backing vocals. AudioJoy wrote the other verses and the chorus, and sang lead. Collin brought a brilliant rap, backing vocals, and the arranger's ear. Matt Pop turned the tune into a bright, spangly anthem of sass — with a sparkle borrowed, in part, from Pippa Dee.

  • Kieran — original music, verse, backing vocals

  • Collin — rap, backing vocals, arrangement

About

AudioJoy is a songwriting project built around other people’s voices. I write; I don’t front. The songs are made for performers who can take a lyric and turn it into a world.

The 2023 single Shook By The Look was a co-write with Kieran and Collin, which Matt Pop turned into a bright, spangly anthem of sass — with a sparkle borrowed, in part, from my friend Pippa Dee.

AudioJoy is a collective now. You’ll be hearing about our powerhouse 8-octave-range vocalist soon.
