Shook By The Look

An anthem of sass

A co-write, and the only song the three of us made together. Kieran created the original music, wrote a verse, and sang backing vocals. I wrote the other verses and the chorus, and sang lead. Collin brought a brilliant rap, backing vocals, and the arranger’s ear. Matt Pop turned the tune into a bright, spangly anthem of sass — with a sparkle borrowed, in part, from Pippa Dee.

  • Kieran — original music, verse, backing vocals
  • AudioJoy — lead vocals, verses, chorus
  • Collin — rap, backing vocals, arrangement
  • Matt Pop — the bright, spangly anthem

Artist bio

For the page.

I’m a British queer pop writer working from the wings. I write to put songs on bodies that can hold a stage — drag artists, vocalists, performers who can take a look and make it a world.

The 2023 single “Shook By The Look” is a co-write with Kieran and Collin. Matt Pop turned the tune into a bright, spangly anthem of sass — a sparkle shaped, in part, by my friend Pippa Dee, a special kind of angel who worked the door at London’s Royal Vauxhall Tavern.

My forthcoming suite, Yellow Aura, is dreamy pop in gold, written for a voice still to be found. What exists now is a sketch, not a master.

Dedication

For Pippa Dee. A special kind of angel. For many years she worked the door at the Royal Vauxhall Tavern — the threshold of the night, the first warmth, the person who could make a doorway feel like you were already loved. Some of the sparkle in this song is hers. I love her forever.
