Newsflash · October 2026

Someone Somewhere is moving to the new year.

It was due on 6 November. It’ll now be January, possibly February.

The honest reason is that the song changed. What began as one idea has become something else — which is the best kind of surprise, and also the kind that needs more time. Rushing it to hit a date would serve the date, not the song.

The charity beneficiary for the Song 4 Pippa mix is being reconsidered too. The intention is unchanged: proceeds, after costs, go to a charity supporting orphans and widows. I’m taking the time to find the right one.

Anyone on the list hears the new date first.

An anthem of sass

A co-write, and the only song the three of us made together. Kieran created the original music, wrote a verse, and sang backing vocals. I wrote the other verses and the chorus, and sang lead. Collin brought a brilliant rap, backing vocals, and the arranger's ear. Matt Pop turned the tune into a bright, spangly anthem of sass — with a sparkle borrowed, in part, from Pippa Dee.
